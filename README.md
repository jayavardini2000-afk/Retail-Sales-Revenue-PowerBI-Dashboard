## 🚀 Executive Summary
This project delivers an interactive business intelligence solution built on multi-year retail transactional data (2023–2025). It bridges high-level executive KPIs with granular product and customer profitability insights, helping stakeholders identify revenue drivers, optimize sales channels, and evaluate regional performance.

* **Total Revenue:** $242.05K
* **Total Profit:** $87.48K
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 36.14%
* **Total Units Sold:** 5,213

## 🖼️ Dashboard Preview & Architecture

### Page 1: Executive Overview
![Page 1 Executive Overview](page1_executive_overview.png)

* **Purpose:** Acts as an executive command center for high-level stakeholders to monitor financial health at a glance.
* **Key Components:**
  * **Top KPI Card Row:** Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, and AOV.
  * **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Area/line chart tracking multi-year performance volatility.
  * **Categorical & Regional Breakdowns:** Bar charts analyzing revenue distribution by product category and geographic region.
  * **Sales Channel Split:** Donut chart evaluating Online, In-Store, and Marketplace revenue share.
  * **Global Slicers:** Interactive filters for Year, Category, and Region syncing seamlessly across pages.

### Page 2: Product & Customer Analysis
![Page 2 Product & Customer Analysis](page2_product_customer_analysis.png)

* **Purpose:** Empowers supply chain and sales teams to drill down into product-level margins and high-value customer concentration.
* **Key Components:**
  * **Category & Product Matrix:** Drill-down table enhanced with **Slate Teal data bars** on Profit Margin %.
  * **Top 10 Products by Revenue:** Clustered bar chart highlighting best-selling product lines.
  * **Top 10 Customers by Revenue:** Customer concentration and VIP buyer analysis.
  * **Volume vs. Profitability Scatter Plot:** Advanced analytical visual mapping product quantities against total profit grouped by category.

## 🎨 Design System & UI/UX Standards
To ensure enterprise-ready visual hierarchy, the report follows a strict corporate design framework:
* **Primary Header & Accents:** Midnight Navy (`#1E3A8A`)
* **Data Highlights & Visual Bars:** Slate Teal (`#0D9488`)
* **Canvas Background:** Soft Cool Gray (`#F4F6F9`) for clean card elevation and depth.
* **Typography:** Standardized Calibri sans-serif font architecture with controlled text scaling.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling & Transformation:** Star schema architecture, calculated columns, and explicit DAX measures.
* **Visual Formatting:** Custom conditional formatting, data bars, dynamic top-N filters, and synchronized cross-page slicers.
* **Analytical Techniques:** Revenue concentration analysis, profit margin variance tracking, and scatter plot distribution modeling.
